# Figures Guide

Thu muc nay luu toan bo artifact hinh anh cua project.

## Nhom hinh hien tai

- Top-level PNG files:
  artifact tong hop tu exploratory EDA overview
- `eda_chart/`:
  cac hinh exploratory tap trung vao delay analysis
- `risk_analysis/`:
  cac hinh exploratory tap trung vao cancellation, diversion va risk analysis

## Quy uoc su dung

- Neu tao hinh tong quan moi, uu tien dat ten ro rang o top-level `reports/figures/`
- Neu tao hinh tu notebook exploratory, dat trong thu muc chu de rieng
- Khong tron artifact dashboard, modeling va exploratory vao cung mot nhom khi chua can thiet
